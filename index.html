What gets randomized?

Eligible combat characters can receive a different name, portrait, appearance, and starting setup. Story events and dialogue follow the original characters.

Can I continue an existing save?

Existing randomized saves keep their saved seed and cast. Start a New Game to use a different roster, seed, or start point. Ordinary saves cannot be converted into randomized runs.

Are any characters excluded?

Ted, Odessa, and non-combat characters stay outside the pools. You can include Tir in Character Shuffle. Tir has a reserved slot in both six-hero roster modes.

How do six-hero rosters work?

Choose your five companions or let the seed choose them. Tir always joins them. Your active party can have fewer than six heroes while the story is still introducing party members.

Why are some Unite attacks unavailable?

Some original combinations are disabled when their characters have changed, to avoid incompatible attacks.
