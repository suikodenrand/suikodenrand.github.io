YOUR CAST, YOUR CAMPAIGN

A familiar world, reshuffled.

Both Suikoden adventures

Seeded character shuffle, five chosen companions, or a random fixed roster. Each game keeps its own settings and saved campaign.

Names and native Unites

Supported English dialogue mentions follow shuffled names. Native Unite eligibility follows the fighters in your party, including replacements in unrelated story roles.

Joining weapons that fit

Default joining weapon levels follow Tir in S1 or Riou in S2, with a seeded ±3 variation. Original-role and level 1 options are available.

One settings launcher

Manage seeds, rosters, quick starts, presets and included Suikoden Fix options. F6 starts hidden; its visible views use text without a black box.

S1 treasure shuffle Experimental

Optional campaign-seeded rewards for ordinary item chests. Disabled by default; special and progression-sensitive rewards stay original.

S1 active battles Optional experiment

SPD meters, individual native commands and queued actions. Select ATB in setup, then enable it in the launcher with S1 shuffled Unites disabled. S2 retains turn-based battles.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Ready to set out?

Installation, upgrades, backups and troubleshooting.

Open the setup guide →

Download offline guide ↓

Does this include both games?

Yes. One combined randomizer and launcher manage Suikoden I and II HD Remaster. Each game has separate settings and campaign bindings. You need your own supported Steam Windows copy.

Do I need to install BepInEx separately?

No. This full package includes BepInEx, Suikoden Fix, the launcher and their private runtimes. First launch still needs internet to prepare the Unity libraries.

Can I keep an existing campaign?

Upgrades preserve settings and saves. Existing randomized campaigns retain their seed, cast and joining-weapon policy. Keep Save and BepInEx/config together in a backup. New settings apply to new campaigns.

Are ATB and chest shuffling enabled?

No. S1 chest shuffling is off by default. S1 ATB is an optional installer component and is installed disabled; turn off S1 shuffled Unites before enabling it. Neither experiment is implemented for S2.

How complete is the preview?

Representative battles, shuffled Unites, scene replacements and saves have been tested. Wider campaign and scripted-encounter testing continues. English name replacement covers supported messages, not every line. Nuzlocke is not included.
